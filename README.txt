FLY EXPRESS PASSENGER APP — COMPLETE DEMO

1. Open index.html in a modern browser, preferably through a small local static server.
2. Use OTP 123456, or choose Continue as Guest.
3. Refresh the page to reset every simulated state.
4. Internet access is used only for OpenStreetMap tiles and the icon, QR and barcode libraries. Passenger and form data remain in browser memory.

SUPPLIED MEDIA INCLUDED
- Official Fly Express Travellers Association logo
- Fly Express passenger van
- Browser-ready Entebbe-to-Kampala route MP4
- Supplied 14-seater interior used as the real seat-selector background

BOOKING EXPERIENCE
- Trip search selections carry through to review, checkout, confirmation and ticket.
- The default path stays short: choose a departure and continue.
- Return travel is a one-click toggle on the booking CTA.
- Passengers, assistance, language, luggage and seat choice stay collapsed until needed.
- The trip map uses OpenStreetMap and condenses while the passenger scrolls.
- Fourteen transparent passenger-seat hotspots sit over the supplied vehicle image.
- The top-right driver seat is visible but disabled.

INCLUDED JOURNEYS
Splash, onboarding, sign-in, OTP, registration, home, trip search, progressive trip review,
capacity and seat selection, one-way and return tickets, luggage, checkout, wallet,
mobile-money and cash previews, booking success, digital ticket, trip history, live trip,
eight-step parcel booking, receipt and tracking, offers, advertisements, notifications,
support, lost property, profile, language and accessibility settings, About and demo states.

This is a front-end demonstration. It does not create real bookings, move money, send messages or store account data.
